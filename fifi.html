FIFI

A tooth fairy keepsake about memory and time.

01Overview

Background

This design is meant to act as a physical reminder of those moments, allowing donors to reconnect with a meaningful memory through interaction.

I was tasked with creating a product for Infinitum that explores memory and time. Every person carries special memories tied to specific moments in their life and experiences that shape who they are. Chose six images that related most to time and memory. Each piece captures a certain time in everybody’s memory. The bus and paper airplane relate to childhood experiences. The leaves, hair, fossils, and faded photo show how things start to change and get old throughout time.

I have decided to create an idea based on the tooth fairy, a childhood memory in which a fairy places money under a child’s bed in exchange for a tooth.

04Final Prototype

How to use

  • Put the tooth in the top slit
  • Spin the tooth to whichever drawer you want the tooth to be put in
  • Take the tooth off the stand and put it next to the child
  • Put money into the slit of the tooth.
