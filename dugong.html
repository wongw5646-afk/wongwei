Dugong

01Background

We were asked by the ( ) to create a product that best represents the poses of endangered animals. I decided to choose the Amur leopard and dugong. The reason for that it because of how different they both are in terms of personality and habitat. The Amur leopard is located on land and is a very assertive animal, while a dugong lives in the water and relaxes all day long.

02Sketches

Amur Leopard

Dugong

Foam Models
