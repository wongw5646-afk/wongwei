Vessel

A paper lantern that fills modern homes with soft, warm light.

01Overview

Background

I was tasked with creating a light fixture for IKEA. Lighting can come in different shapes and sizes and be placed in certain areas. It is meant for people who appreciate modern designs and want their lighting to not only be functional but also enhance the mood of their space. By combining aesthetics with warmth, this light helps turn a house into a home.
